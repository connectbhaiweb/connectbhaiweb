## What is in the database

- **60 customers**, each with exactly one connection request (lead). Every ticket, chat, payment and install below belongs to one of them.
- **16 DSAs** (they only bring leads, no fees, earn a flat payout when their lead is installed) and **21 TIPs** (installers, incl. the internal Connect Bhai TIP).
- Leads by status: New 10, Contacted 8, Follow-up 7, Booked 6, Installed 22, Lost 4, Cancelled 3.
